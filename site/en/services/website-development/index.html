Questions before you order

How much does a website cost?

A landing page starts from 250,000 ₸ and a corporate website from 500,000 ₸. The final price depends on the number of pages, design, content, features and integrations. We prepare an estimate after a short description of your project.

Why do website prices differ so much?

Price depends on the website type, custom design or a ready template, the number of pages, copywriting, forms, integrations and the complexity of the logic. Compare offers from several studios only for the same scope of work.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes 2–4 weeks. A corporate website takes 4–8 weeks if materials are ready and 2–3 months if design and content are made from scratch. A web service with accounts takes from 3 months. It goes faster when structure, texts, photos and access are ready, and slower when approvals drag on.

How is a landing page different from a corporate website?

A landing page is one page for a specific offer or ad. A corporate website has several pages: services, about the company, projects and contacts. It suits search better when you have several lines of business.

What does basic SEO setup include?

Clear page URLs, titles and descriptions, the main site address, a sitemap and access for search robots. Indexing and positions are decided by search engines.

Can I order a website without ready texts and design?

Yes. We start with the structure, visitor tasks and materials about your company. Content and design are included in the agreed scope of work.

A builder like Tilda or custom development?

A builder suits a quick launch and a simple site you edit yourself. Custom development is needed for integrations, accounts and full control over speed and SEO. We suggest an option after reviewing the task.

Can the website be in Russian and Kazakh?

Yes, but plan the languages from the start: page URLs, a switcher and language version markup. Texts in two languages increase the scope, so we include them in the estimate.

Can you connect a CRM, analytics and notifications?

Yes, if the service provides a way to connect. Enquiries can go to staff on Telegram, to a CRM or by email, and analytics can use visit and event counters.

How much does support after launch cost?

It depends on the scope: content edits, updates, monitoring, new pages. We discuss it separately and fix it in advance so costs after launch are not a surprise.
