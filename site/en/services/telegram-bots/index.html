BOOKING · OUR OWN PROJECT

Salon Booking

From choosing a service to confirming the booking, all inside Telegram.

Code on GitHub ↗

The Mini App leads the customer through the steps: service → specialist → day and free time → booking details. After submission the bot confirms the booking, and the administrator approves or declines it.

  • Free slots are requested from the server.
  • The customer sees upcoming bookings and can cancel them.
  • Reminders 24 and 2 hours before the visit.
  • The administrator gets the schedule for today and tomorrow.

What we built: Mini App interface, API, booking logic, database and bot. Java 17, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL.

Bookings currently use hourly slots. Rescheduling is done by cancelling and booking again. This is our own project, not a deployment for a working salon.

TASKS AND REMINDERS · OUR OWN PROJECT

Reminder Bot · “My Home”

Personal and shared tasks with a schedule and reminders.

Code on GitHub ↗

A user creates a task, sets a date or repeat, receives a Telegram reminder and marks it done. The Mini App shows upcoming tasks, a calendar and progress.

Questions before you order

What should I write to get an estimate?

Who will use the bot, which action needs automating and what the end result should be. Add examples of messages, tables or the current process, and mention your CRM or website if you have one. A budget and a desired date help us define the first version.

How much does a bot cost?

A bot starts from 70,000 ₸ and a Mini App from 500,000 ₸. The final price depends on features: dialogue, schedule, permissions, integrations and launch. For an enquiry form we need the fields and the recipient; for booking, also the schedule, taken slots and cancellation.

How long does it take?

A bot for enquiries and notifications takes 1–2 weeks. Booking, tasks, roles and a schedule take 3–5 weeks. A Mini App with integrations takes 4–8 weeks or more. We fix the timeline after agreeing the scope.

Do I have to build a Mini App?

No. For an enquiry, notifications or delivering materials, messages and buttons are enough. A Mini App makes sense when users need a calendar, a catalogue, several connected screens or an account.

Can we start with a simple bot and add features later?

Yes, and we recommend it. We launch the main scenario, look at real enquiries, then add CRM, payments or a Mini App. You pay only for what you actually need.

Can you connect CRM and payments?

Yes, if the chosen services provide a way to connect. Before work starts we check API availability, account limits and the payment provider. Fees and services of external systems are not included in the development price.

Does the bot work in Russian and Kazakh?

Yes, texts can be prepared in both languages. We agree the languages and how users switch between them before development, because they affect the scope.

What do I pay for every month?

It depends on hosting: usually a server, a database and the paid services the bot uses. We define the list of costs in advance. Support and further development are a separately agreed scope.

Who keeps the code and access?

We hand over the source code, instructions and access to you. The bot and hosting can be registered under your accounts so you stay in control of the service.

Can you improve an existing bot?

Yes, after studying the source code, hosting and integrations. First we decide what can be kept and what has to change, then we estimate the work.
