Questions before you order

How is a Mini App different from a Telegram bot?

A bot communicates with messages and buttons. A Mini App provides a web interface inside Telegram: forms, lists, a calendar and navigation. They often work together.

How much does Mini App development cost?

Mini App development starts from 500,000 ₸. The final price depends on screens, user roles, back-end logic and integrations. First we agree on scenarios and the limits of the first version, then the price and stages.

How long does Mini App development take?

For the first version, expect 4–8 weeks. The timeline grows with the number of screens, roles and integrations. We fix the exact timeline after agreeing on the scope.

Do users need to install a separate app?

A Mini App opens from Telegram. Users do not need to install a separate app from a store.

When is a bot enough and when do you need a Mini App?

A bot is enough for an enquiry, notifications or delivering materials. You need a Mini App when users need a calendar, a catalogue, several connected screens or an account.

Can we start with a bot and add a Mini App later?

Yes. That is a common path: launch a bot with the main scenario, see how people use it, and add the app when an interface with several screens becomes necessary.

Can you connect payments and external systems?

Yes, if the chosen service provides a way to connect. We check payments, CRM and accounting systems before work starts. Fees and services of external systems are not included in the development price.

What should I prepare for an estimate?

Tell us who will use the app, which actions are needed and what the result should be. Attach examples of screens or processes, name the systems to connect, and give the desired deadline and budget.
